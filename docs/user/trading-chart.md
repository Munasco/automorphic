# Trading from the chart

Right-click a price and choose Buy, Sell or Add order to open an order ticket. Select an account from your connected Tradovate environment, review whether it is Demo or Live, and enter the quantity and order details. The ticket supports market, limit, stop and stop-limit orders with Day or GTC duration. An order is sent only when you submit the ticket. Orders are unavailable during replay.

If an order's status is unknown, check Orders in Tradovate before opening another ticket. Repeating the same submission does not send another order. A confirmed submission means Tradovate accepted the order; it does not mean the order has filled.

Use Lock vertical cursor line by time in the chart's right-click menu to keep the crosshair on a chosen bar while comparing prices. Select it again to unlock. Drawing and indicator removal commands show how many items they will remove.
