# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
Disagreement about code is welcome. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct,
and publishing another person's private information are not.

Respect contributors' time and boundaries. Critique the work, explain the problem, and give
others room to correct mistakes. These expectations apply to everyone representing the project.

Report misconduct privately to [munachiernesteze@gmail.com](mailto:munachiernesteze@gmail.com).
Maintainers may remove content, close conversations, or restrict participation when necessary.
Reports will be handled as privately as practical; do not include unrelated personal information.
